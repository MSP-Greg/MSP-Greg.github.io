      Overview

      The joins of a relation, grouped by how they are built into the query.

      #named_joins are association names, built into one join dependency rooted at the model. #stashed_joins are join dependencies that are built already, joined in that order after it. #leading_joins and #join_nodes are ::Arel nodes, joined before and after both.
